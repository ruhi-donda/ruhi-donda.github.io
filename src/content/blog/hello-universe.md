I have wanted a personal site for years. Not a LinkedIn, not a link-in-bio — an
actual place I control, where I can write badly on purpose and change my mind in
public.

So here it is. Consider this the ground-floor lobby of a building I'll keep
adding rooms to.

## What lives here

- **Projects** — the things I build when I should be sleeping.
- **Writing** — half-formed thoughts about AI, space, and whatever I'm nerding
  out on this month.
- **Hobbies** — Blender renders, art, photos, and the slow victory of keeping
  plants alive.

## Why "space"?

Because the night sky is the original open-source project: freely available,
absurdly well-documented, and nobody can gatekeep it. Also it looks cool.

More soon. Thanks for orbiting. 🛰️
